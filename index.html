Lovely Clan Bot

A Discord bot and live stats site for the Lovely Clash of Clans clan, built on the official Clash of Clans API.

  • Records every war and CWL attack: 3-star rates, average stars, defense hold rates and lifetime donations for each member.
  • Every command answers with a rendered image card (war line-ups, leaderboards, player profiles) with custom Town Hall badge art.
  • Pings linked members who still have attacks left an hour and 30 minutes before a war ends.
  • Hand-picked war and CWL rosters, plus /makewar, which builds a 5–25 a side line-up from everyone's war preference.

Workout Scribe

A Strong-style iPhone workout log that knows how strong you should be today, not just what you lifted last time.

  • Gold "stronger than expected" sets: a set turns gold when it beats what last time predicts.
  • Bench-angle strength curve: log the incline per set, and it converts strength between angles.
  • Fatigue-aware suggestions: exercise order and earlier sets adjust today's targets.
  • Muscle recovery by training split, with a home-screen widget.
  • Built-in rest timer, live on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island.
  • Free iCloud sync, no ads, and import of your history from Strong.

Temu Kahoot

A Kahoot-style quiz for family game night. There's no server: the host's browser is the game, and phones join peer to peer over WebRTC.

  • Players join with a 4-letter code that's easy to read aloud across the room.
  • Quizzes are plain CSV files, so a new set is one quick edit on GitHub.
  • Faster right answers score more, with answer bars and a podium at the end.
