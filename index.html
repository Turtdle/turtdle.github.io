Lovely Clan Bot

A Discord bot and live stats site for the Lovely Clash of Clans clan, built on the official Clash of Clans API.

  • Records every war and CWL attack: 3-star rates, average stars, defense hold rates and lifetime donations for each member.
  • Every command answers with a rendered image card (war line-ups, leaderboards, player profiles) with custom Town Hall badge art.
  • Pings linked members who still have attacks left an hour and 30 minutes before a war ends.
  • Hand-picked war and CWL rosters, plus /makewar, which builds a 5–25 a side line-up from everyone's war preference.

Workout Scribe

A Strong-style iPhone workout log that knows how strong you should be today, not just what you lifted last time.

  • Gold sets that mean something: each set is compared with what you should lift today, adjusted for bench angle and for fatigue from earlier exercises.
  • Bench-angle strength curve: incline sets convert to a flat-bench equivalent, so 30° and flat progress share one chart.
  • Fatigue-aware suggestions: it estimates strength lost to exercise order, based on published research, and scales your targets to match.
  • Muscle recovery for main and helper muscles, grouped by your split (PPL, Upper/Lower, Arnold…), with a home-screen widget.
  • A rest timer built into the set, with a Lock Screen and Dynamic Island Live Activity.
  • Free iCloud sync with no account and no ads, plus Strong import, a plate calculator and automatic warm-up sets.

Temu Kahoot

A Kahoot-style quiz for family game night. There's no server: the host's browser is the game, and phones join peer to peer over WebRTC.

  • Players join with a 4-letter code that's easy to read aloud across the room.
  • Quizzes are plain CSV files, so a new set is one quick edit on GitHub.
  • Faster right answers score more, with answer bars and a podium at the end.

BrickPoint

A daily LEGO price-guessing game you play in a Discord call or in the browser. Every day brings five sets from one release year. Guess what each one cost at launch.

  • Started as a Discord Activity for pricepoint.gg's daily "guess what it sold for" puzzle. The same code now runs the LEGO edition, with set data from Brickset.
  • Everyone plays at their own pace. A live sidebar shows how each friend in the channel did on every round, but never their guesses.
  • Up to 5,000 points a round, depending on how close you get. Revealing the piece count costs 20% of that round.
  • The bot posts a results card to the channel as people finish, plus a daily recap with streaks and monthly standings.
